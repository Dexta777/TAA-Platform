# The Atelier Glossary

> A shared language for the practice of deliberate craftsmanship.

---

## Purpose

Language shapes thought.

The Atelier maintains a shared vocabulary so that important ideas may be expressed precisely, consistently and without unnecessary ambiguity.

The definitions within this Glossary are not dictionary definitions.

They describe the meaning of each term as it is understood within the Atelier Doctrine.

The vocabulary itself may evolve through rectification.

---

# A

## Architecture

The deliberate organisation of responsibilities within a system.

Architecture is measured not by complexity, but by the clarity with which responsibility is expressed.

---

## Artisan

A practitioner of the Atelier Doctrine.

An artisan contributes through observation, understanding, stewardship and deliberate craftsmanship.

The title is earned through practice rather than granted through hierarchy.

---

## Atelier

A workshop devoted to continual refinement.

The Atelier represents both a philosophy and a place in which thoughtful engineering is practised.

---

# B

## Boundary

A clearly defined separation of responsibility.

Healthy boundaries reduce confusion, preserve understanding and encourage coherent evolution.

---

# C

## Coagulation

The temporary state reached after successful rectification.

A system is considered coagulated when it is stable enough to support continued evolution.

Coagulation is never permanent.

Every stable state eventually becomes the beginning of another cycle of refinement.

---

## Coherence

The primary measure of quality within the Atelier.

A coherent system communicates its intent clearly, separates responsibility appropriately and remains understandable as it evolves.

---

## Codex

The living body of knowledge accompanying a repository.

The Codex preserves understanding, intent and accumulated experience alongside the implementation itself.

---

## Compact

A concise statement of shared commitment.

Compacts summarise principles without replacing the Doctrine from which they emerge.

---

# D

## Decision

A deliberate choice made through observation, understanding and judgement.

Important decisions should preserve their reasoning through Architecture Decision Records.

---

## Doctrine

The enduring body of principles that governs how judgement is exercised.

The Doctrine teaches how to think rather than what to think.

---

# E

## Evolution

The continual improvement of a system through deliberate refinement.

Evolution values continuity over disruption.

---

## Explicitness

The practice of making important intent visible.

Explicit systems reduce hidden assumptions and unnecessary ambiguity.

---

# G

## Glossary

The shared vocabulary of the Atelier.

It exists to preserve precision of language and consistency of thought.

---

# I

## Inquiry

The disciplined investigation of an observation.

Inquiry precedes understanding.

---

# J

## Judgement

The thoughtful application of knowledge within a specific context.

Judgement cannot be replaced by rules alone.

---

# L

## Leaf

A significant milestone in the intellectual or architectural evolution of a project.

Leaves record meaningful progress rather than routine activity.

---

# O

## Observation

The deliberate act of noticing.

Every rectification begins with careful observation.

---

## Observatory

The place where ideas, questions, experiments and possibilities are collected before they mature into doctrine or implementation.

Not every observation belongs immediately within the Doctrine.

---

# R

## Rectification

The deliberate refinement of an existing artefact.

Rectification seeks greater coherence without unnecessary disruption.

It may affect software, documentation, architecture or understanding itself.

---

## Responsibility

The purpose entrusted to a component, document or individual.

A responsibility should have one clear owner.

---

# S

## Stewardship

The practice of caring for a system so that it remains understandable, maintainable and capable of future evolution.

Stewardship values continuity above ownership.

---

# U

## Understanding

Knowledge that has been tested through observation and thoughtful inquiry.

Understanding precedes confident action.

---

# W

## Workshop

The collaborative environment in which artisans practise their craft.

The Atelier extends an open workshop.

Participation is earned through contribution rather than affiliation.

---

## Living Language

The vocabulary of the Atelier is expected to evolve.

New terms should emerge naturally through practice.

Definitions should become clearer through rectification rather than larger through accumulation.

The language exists to increase understanding.

Whenever it becomes a source of confusion, it should itself be rectified.
